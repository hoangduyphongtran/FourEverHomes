Our Process

Built on a JobTread Workflow

Every project runs through JobTread, our construction management platform — keeping subcontractors, vendors, and clients connected in one system from first estimate to final walkthrough. Nothing falls through the cracks.

01

Estimate & Bid

We build the job budget and send bid requests to subs directly through JobTread's vendor portal.

02

Purchase Orders

Approved bids convert into POs and work orders, so every sub knows scope, cost, and timeline.

03

Daily Logs

Field progress, photos, and schedule updates are logged in real time and shared across the team.

04

Client Portal

You track progress, approve selections, and view documents through your own client portal.

05

Closeout

Change orders, invoices, and warranties are tracked to close every job clean and transparent.
